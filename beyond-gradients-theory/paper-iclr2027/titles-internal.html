Title Decision Log

Paper: The Outer Loop (ICLR 2027) · Created 2026-06-12 · Update when title changes
Chosen title (tentative)
The Outer Loop: A Unified Theory of Gradient-Free LLM Improvement Loops
Documented alternate: "The Ratchet Illusion: Resources, Certificates, and Limits of LLM-Guided Artifact Search" — held in reserve as the provocative/citation-shorthand variant.

Rationale for chosen title

"The Outer Loop" coins the category name — the pattern every frozen-weight self-improvement system runs. It is short, memorable, and creates a natural citation handle ("Outer Loop Theory" or just "the outer loop paper"). The subtitle is descriptive and searchable. It does not duplicate the Weng (2026) blog title (which uses "beyond gradients", already avoided in v2.5).

The competitor was "The Ratchet Illusion" as lead phrase. That is stronger as a hook but risks the paper being remembered only for the ratchet result (§5), when the theory is broader. Decision: keep "ratchet illusion" as a concept name within the paper and as an alternate title option; use "The Outer Loop" as the submission title.

Provocative / Memorable

✓ Documented alternate
The Ratchet Illusion: Resources, Certificates, and Limits of LLM-Guided Artifact Search

Provocative number-lead. Too narrow — sounds like a measurement paper, not a theory paper.

Dual / Subtitle format

✓ Chosen

Dual

Dual

"Beyond Prompt Tuning" searchable from prompt engineering community. Might anchor expectations wrong.
